Mango Float

Description

Mango Float is a popular Filipino no-bake dessert made with layers of ripe mangoes, graham crackers, and sweetened cream. It is chilled until firm and served cold.

Ingredients

  • 2 packs graham crackers
  • 2 cups chilled all-purpose cream
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 3 to 4 ripe mangoes, sliced
  • Crushed graham crackers for topping

Steps

  1. Wash, peel, and slice the ripe mangoes.
  2. Mix the chilled all-purpose cream and condensed milk.
  3. Arrange a layer of graham crackers in a container.
  4. Spread a layer of cream mixture over the crackers.
  5. Add a layer of sliced mangoes.
  6. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  7. Top with crushed graham crackers and mango slices.
  8. Cover and refrigerate until firm.
  9. Serve chilled.
