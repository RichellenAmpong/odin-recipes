Sinigang na Baboy

Home

Description

Sinigang na Baboy is a traditional Filipino sour soup made with pork, vegetables, and a souring ingredient such as tamarind. It is commonly served hot with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or pork ribs
  • 1 packet tamarind soup mix
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong
  • 6 cups water
  • Fish sauce to taste

Steps

  1. Place the pork in a pot with water.
  2. Bring to a boil and remove any foam from the surface.
  3. Add the onion and tomatoes.
  4. Simmer until the pork becomes tender.
  5. Add the radish and eggplant.
  6. Add the tamarind soup mix and stir well.
  7. Season with fish sauce according to taste.
  8. Add the kangkong and cook until tender.
  9. Serve hot with steamed rice.
