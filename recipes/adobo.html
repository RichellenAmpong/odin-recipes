Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black pepper. It is known for its delicious savory and slightly sour flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Wash and prepare the chicken.
  2. Mix soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and black pepper.
  3. Marinate the chicken for 30 minutes.
  4. Place the chicken and marinade in a pot.
  5. Add water and bay leaves.
  6. Simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.
  7. Serve hot with steamed rice.
